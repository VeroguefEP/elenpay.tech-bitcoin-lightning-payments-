Lightning payment gateway vs. standard crypto payment gateway

Standard crypto payment gateways process on-chain Bitcoin transactions, typically taking 10 to 60 minutes to confirm, with fees that fluctuate under congestion. ElenPAY's Lightning payment gateway settles on the Lightning Network, a Layer 2 protocol built on top of Bitcoin, processing transactions in under 1 second at network fees of less than $0.01 per transaction.

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions from our clients and partners. You can also learn more about Lightning payments by visiting our Lightning Knowledge Hub or reaching out to our team.

What is a crypto payment gateway?+
A crypto payment gateway is a software infrastructure that enables businesses to accept cryptocurrency payments from customers, convert them into confirmed payments, and credit funds to the merchant's account. Unlike traditional payment gateways that process card or bank transfers, crypto gateways settle directly on blockchain or Layer 2 protocols such as the Lightning Network, eliminating intermediaries and the delays they introduce.
How does ElenPAY's crypto payment gateway work?+
ElenPAY's payment gateway processes Bitcoin Lightning payments in three steps: a Lightning invoice is automatically generated when a customer initiates payment; the transaction settles through the Lightning Network in under one second; and Bitcoin is credited to the merchant's back office in real time. There are no clearing windows or float; funds are available the moment the customer pays.
What integration methods does ElenPAY offer?+
ElenPAY offers five integration methods to fit different technical environments and go-live timelines: a REST API for full backend control over the payment flow; an embeddable payment widget for fast deployment with minimal development effort; payment links for no-code payment collection without any development requirement; a payment aggregator and white-label option for businesses that want to launch Lightning payments under their own brand; and integration through ElenPAY's PSP Partnerships program, which enables established payment providers and distributors to offer Lightning payments within their existing ecosystems.
How long does it take to integrate ElenPAY's Lightning payment gateway?+
Integration time depends on the method chosen. The REST API is designed to be integrated in hours, not weeks, with a sandbox environment for testing before going live. The payment widget can be embedded with minimal development effort, making it the fastest path to accepting Lightning payments on an existing platform. Payment links require only backend access to generate a payment URL with no frontend development needed. ElenPAY provides expert integration guidance and 24/7 technical support throughout the process.
Does ElenPAY support pay-ins and payouts?+
Yes. ElenPAY supports both pay-ins (receiving payments from customers) and payouts (sending funds, or withdrawals, to users, affiliates, or partners). Mass payouts can be processed simultaneously, making ElenPAY suitable for platforms with large affiliate or partner networks, gaming platforms with frequent player withdrawals, and marketplaces that distribute earnings to multiple recipients.
Is ElenPAY's payment gateway suitable for iGaming platforms?+
Yes. ElenPAY's gateway is purpose-built for high-frequency, real-time deposit and withdrawal flows required by iGaming and online casino platforms. Lightning's sub-1-second settlement enables instant player deposits, while zero chargebacks eliminate a major operational risk for operators. ElenPAY also supports the operational requirements of international gaming markets.
Can ElenPAY be used as a white-label payment gateway?+
Yes. ElenPAY offers a white-label Lightning PSP option, enabling businesses to launch their own Lightning payment service under their brand. ElenPAY manages the underlying node infrastructure, liquidity management, and routing. The operator owns the customer relationship and frontend experience. This is designed for PSPs, neobanks, and fintech platforms that want to add Lightning payments as a proprietary product.
