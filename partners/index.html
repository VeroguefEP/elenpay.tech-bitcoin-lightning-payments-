Frequently Asked Questions

How the ElenPAY partner network works, who it's for, and what merchants can expect.

The ElenPAY PSP Partner Network is a directory of certified payment service providers that have integrated ElenPAY's Bitcoin Lightning infrastructure into their payment stacks. Through these partners, merchants and platforms can access Lightning payment services locally or regionally, with the support of a team that already knows the integration.
The partner network serves two audiences. First, businesses that want to accept Bitcoin Lightning payments through a PSP they already work with, or through a regional provider that fits their compliance and operational context. Second, PSPs and payment companies that want to add Lightning to their payment rails using ElenPAY's infrastructure.
All network partners use ElenPAY's Lightning infrastructure, which means sub-1-second settlement, fees below 1%, zero chargebacks, and access to 650M+ Lightning-compatible wallets globally. Each partner handles onboarding, support, and commercial terms independently. ElenPAY provides the underlying payment rail.
Direct integration with ElenPAY gives you full access to the API, widget, and infrastructure under your own account. Integrating through a partner means working with a PSP that has already built the Lightning layer into their platform — often better suited for businesses that prefer managed services, have an existing relationship with that PSP, or need regional support.

Onboarding is straightforward — ElenPAY handles the infrastructure so you can focus on your merchants.
